## Boilerplate (approved facts only)

**One line:** MENVA is a Lahore startup that lets diners see a restaurant's real dishes in 3D, on their own table at true size, before they order.

**Short (≈60 words):** MENVA turns a restaurant's table QR into a 3D menu. Diners scan the code, tap a dish and see a real 3D scan of the plate, then place it on their own table in augmented reality at its true size. There's no app to install: it works in Safari and Chrome. The waiter still takes the order. MENVA is running its first pilots in Lahore.

**Facts:**
- Real scans only: no AI-generated food images, ever.
- True size: in AR the dish can't be pinched bigger, so the portion is honest.
- Built for restaurant wifi: the dish photo shows first, and the 3D follows.
- No online ordering or payment: guests build a "show the waiter" list.
- Founder: Abdullah. Based in Lahore.

**Don't state** (not confirmed yet): the names of pilot restaurants, what the pilot includes, any results or numbers. For those, ask Abdullah.
