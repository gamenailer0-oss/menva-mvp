The four restaurant carousels from the Instagram calendar, as PDFs. On LinkedIn: **Start a post → Add a document → upload the PDF**, give it a title, and it shows as a swipeable carousel. Pair each one with a founder post from `social/marketing/channels.md` §3.

| File | Pairs well with | Title to give it |
|---|---|---|
| `06-quietest-salesperson.pdf` | A post about why menus that only describe food lose orders | Your menu is your quietest salesperson |
| `26-owners-faq-1.pdf` | "The four questions every owner asks me" | Four questions restaurant owners ask about 3D menus |
| `36-owners-how-pilot-works.pdf` | Announcing that Lahore pilots are open | How the MENVA pilot works |
| `46-owners-myths.pdf` | A myth-busting post | Three myths about 3D menus |

Post them a week or more after the Instagram version (#6 goes out on 11 Oct, #26 on 8 Nov, #36 on 22 Nov, #46 on 6 Dec), so the two audiences don't overlap on the same day.
