Owners ask

What owners ask first.

Do guests need an app?

No. The table QR opens the menu in Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.

Does it replace my staff or my menu?

No. There's no online ordering or payment. Guests look, build a list, and your waiter takes the order.

Will it work on our wifi?

It's built for slow restaurant wifi. The dish photo appears first, then the 3D arrives.

What if a guest's phone doesn't support AR?

They can still turn the dish around in 3D. Only the "on the table" view needs an AR-capable phone.

Is it AI-generated?

Never. Every dish is a scan of a real plate from your kitchen.

What does the pilot include?
