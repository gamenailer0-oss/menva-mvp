# Real creator cameos for Madam ki Class

We never copy a real creator's face, voice or catchphrase (that is impersonation, and a brand account doing it gets takedowns or legal notices). Instead, we ask them to appear. Five seconds of a real creator in a Madam ki Class Reel is worth more than any imitation.

## Who to ask
Pick creators whose audience is Lahore or Karachi students and young professionals who eat out: comedians, food vloggers, gamers with a big local following. Check each one's current collab terms on their profile or through their manager; many list a business email or a WhatsApp for collabs. Start with 5 to 10 small and mid-sized creators; big names take longer and cost more.

## What we offer
- A **barter**: dinner for them and friends at a partner restaurant, plus MENVA Black free as a founding member. Money only if they ask and the budget allows.
- Credit: we tag them in the Reel and the caption, and they can post it too (a Collab post puts it on both profiles).
- It's easy: one voice note or one 5 to 10 second selfie video, filmed on their phone.

## The DM (Roman Urdu + English, edit per person)
```
Salam [name]! Hum MENVA hain, Lahore ka ek naya food startup.
Hamari Instagram series "Madam ki Class" mein ek dost khane ki ghalti pe tuition teacher ban jati hai (animated skit).
Kya aap ek chhoti si cameo karna chahenge? Sirf 5-10 second ki video ya voice note, apne style mein.
Badle mein: aap aur doston ka dinner humari taraf se, aur MENVA Black free (founding member).
Reel mein aapko tag karenge, aur Collab post bhi kar sakte hain.
Interested hon to bataiye, script bhej dete hain.
```

## Cameo slots (they say it their own way)
1. **The guest who gets caught:** they walk into the scene, get Madam's "Class se bahar!", and look at the camera: "Main to bas khana khane aaya tha."
2. **The rescuer:** mid-scolding, they cut in: "Madam, aaj chhutti hai." Madam: "Tum bhi murga ban jao."
3. **The one who knows the answer:** they almost say the riddle answer, and Madam: "Chup! Answer sirf DM mein."
4. **The end card:** "Apne group ki Ayesha ko bhejo" said by them, to camera.

We animate our four characters around their real clip (their video in a phone frame, or their voice on a guest character with their name on screen, only with written OK).

## Rules
- Written OK (a WhatsApp message is enough) before we use their face, voice or name.
- They see the final cut before it posts.
- No fake quotes, no invented reactions, no "X recommends MENVA" unless they said it.

## They said yes: what to send each creator
```
Shukriya! Bas 3 cheezein chahiye:
1. Ek line WhatsApp pe: "I agree to appear as a cartoon cameo in MENVA's Madam ki Class Reel, with my name, handle and my own voice."
2. Ye lines apne andaaz mein voice notes pe record kar dein (har line alag note, 5-15 second, quiet jagah):
   [the episode's lines go here]
   Apna signature dialogue bhi add kar dein, jaise aap apni videos mein bolte hain.
3. Apni 2-3 photos (sirf look ke liye: hair, glasses, beard, outfit). Hum apne cartoon style mein banayenge.
Final cut post karne se pehle aapko bhejenge.
```
Then follow `social/reels/cameos/README.md`: add them to `consent.json`, save the voice notes as `cameos/<handle>/<line id>.m4a`, and the builder does the rest.
