# MENVA — community management playbook

Internal working document. Scripts marked **"ready to paste"** are public-facing copy, written to go straight into a comment reply or a DM. Everything else on the page (flags, policy notes, escalation rules) is internal.

Built from `BRIEF.md` and a read-only pass over `social/content/calendar.json` (the 72-post Instagram calendar). Follows the same rules as the brief: nothing here states pilot inclusions, names the pilot restaurant, invents a stat, or uses emoji. Voice throughout: calm, warm, a little playful, never cartoonish, sentence case, English first plus one natural Lahori Roman Urdu line.

A note on the two unnamed dishes: the calendar itself only ever calls them "the green plate" and "the trio" because their real names are unconfirmed. This doc uses the same two placeholders — that's not a new name, it's the calendar's own wording.

The Instagram handle is **@eatmenva** (confirmed 25 Sep).

   *Agar koi sach mein sawal hai toh zaroor poochein.*

## 2. DM playbook

Ready-to-paste DM copy. The keyword flow below assumes Instagram's built-in automated/quick replies (set up from the professional account dashboard, or Meta Business Suite). That setup needs account-owner access — see **Needs Abdullah**. Until it's built, whoever is answering DMs manually can paste the same lines turn by turn.

### Keyword: "PILOT" — restaurant qualification flow

**Auto-reply trigger (word "pilot", case-insensitive) — first message:**

> Hi, thanks for reaching out about the MENVA pilot. A few quick questions so we can point you right:
> 1. Restaurant name?
> 2. Area in Lahore?
> 3. Your role (owner / manager / other)?
> 4. How many tables?
> 5. Best day and time for a short call or visit?
>
> Reply here whenever suits you, no rush.
> *Jab waqt mile reply kar dein, jaldi nahi.*

**Manual follow-ups (send one at a time as each answer comes in, if the automation can't hold the full flow):**

- After restaurant name: "Got it, thanks. What area of Lahore are you in?"
- After area: "Thanks. And just so we loop in the right person — are you the owner, a manager, or handling this for someone else?"
- After role: "Perfect. Roughly how many tables does the restaurant have?"
- After table count: "Last one — what's usually a good day and time for a quick call or a short visit to show you the pilot?"
- **Handoff / close:** "Thanks, this is really helpful. I'm passing this straight to Abdullah, our founder, and he'll reach out to lock in a time. The Lahore pilot is PKR 25,000 — he'll go through what's included with you directly."

Never state what the PKR 25,000 includes — that's not known and stays with Abdullah (see brief). Every completed qualification goes to Abdullah as a lead, not booked as a demo by the community manager.

### Diners: "where can I try this"

> Hey! Right now MENVA is live at our pilot restaurant here in Lahore. You can also try the demo on your own table any time — same 3D, just not tied to a real order: [link in bio]. Once you're scanning a table QR at a restaurant, look for the MENVA mark on the menu.
> *Apni table pe demo try kar sakte ho, link bio mein hai.*

If pressed for the restaurant's name:

> We're not naming the restaurant publicly just yet — the demo on our profile shows you exactly how it works in the meantime.

### Creators asking to collaborate

> Thanks for reaching out — we'd love to hear more. Could you share your handle or portfolio and what kind of collaboration you had in mind? I'll pass it straight to Abdullah, our founder, who handles partnerships, and he'll get back to you.
> *Details bhej dein, Abdullah tak pohcha dete hain.*

**Never say:** never agree to terms, payment, free product, or a shoot date on your own — every creator ask routes to Abdullah (see **Needs Abdullah**).

### Press

> Thanks for getting in touch. For anything press-related, I'll connect you directly with Abdullah, our founder — he handles interviews and press requests personally. Could you share your outlet and deadline so I can flag it as urgent if needed?
> *Press ke liye Abdullah khud baat karte hain, thoda detail bhej dein.*

**Never say:** never answer on-the-record questions yourself; never confirm numbers, plans, or client names to a journalist.

Print production and placement at the restaurant needs Abdullah — see **Needs Abdullah**.

### Hashtag

Proposed: **#MenvaMoment** — distinct from the campaign hashtags already used in the calendar (`#menva`, `#3dmenu`, `#3dscan`, etc.), specific to diner-posted AR moments. This hasn't been checked in-app for prior unrelated use; do that once before it goes on print material — see **Needs Abdullah**.

### Consent / permission DM template (send before any repost)

> Hi! We saw your photo from [pilot restaurant / placeholder] and loved it — okay if we repost it on the MENVA page, with credit to your handle? No pressure either way, just checking first.
> *Aap ki photo bohot achi lagi, humari page pe repost kar sakte hain? Credit zaroor denge.*

### Repost policy

- Always ask first via the consent DM above — never repost on the strength of a tag alone.
- Always credit and tag the original poster in the repost caption.
- Treat no reply within a few days as "not yet," not as consent.
- Never repost a photo that shows other diners' faces without confirming everyone visible is okay with it too.
- Never crop or edit a photo in a way that changes what it actually shows.
- Keep a simple running log (outside this file) of who was asked, what they said, and when/if reposted.

## 4. Giveaways and contests

**Every idea below needs an actual restaurant partner willing to fund and honour the prize before it can be announced or run. None of these go live without that — see Needs Abdullah.**

### Ideas that fit Pakistan / Lahore

1. **"Guess the portion"** — post a scanned dish (reusing the existing "quick game" format from the calendar: is it for one, or for sharing?), ask followers to guess in the comments, draw a winner from correct or closest guesses for a dinner voucher at a partner restaurant.
2. **"Caption this, real prize"** — turn an existing caption-this post into an actual contest with a small prize instead of just a pinned comment.
3. **"Tag the restaurant we should scan next" contest** — the calendar already runs this as an organic prompt (post `restaurant-nomination`, day ~95); turning it into a contest (best or most creative nomination wins something) needs a prize partner too.

### Rules template (fill in the brackets before posting)

> **[Promotion name]**
> Sponsor: MENVA [and partner restaurant, once confirmed]
> Eligibility: Lahore residents, 18+, followers of @eatmenva
> How to enter: [exact steps]
> Entry window: [start] to [end], Asia/Karachi time
> Winner selection: [random draw method], announced by [date]
> Prize: [description]. One entry per person. No cash alternative.
> This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram.
> We'll use your Instagram handle and any DM reply only to contact you if you win.

### Instagram's promotion guidelines (research it, cited)

Per Meta's official Instagram Promotion Guidelines ([help.instagram.com/179379842258600](https://help.instagram.com/179379842258600/)):

- The rules above (eligibility, entry method, prize, winner selection) must be visible to entrants — post them in the caption or link to them.
- The exact line **"This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram"** must appear in the rules.
- Instagram explicitly disallows asking people to tag themselves in a photo they don't actually appear in, as an entry method.
- Comply with any Pakistan-specific regulation that applies to the promotion (not researched here — flag to Abdullah if the prize value is large enough to raise a legal question).

### Fairness

- Use a transparent, verifiable random draw (e.g. a screen-recorded random-number pick against numbered comments) rather than a manual or subjective pick — unless the format is explicitly a team judged one (caption contests), and say so plainly in the post.
- Announce and DM the winner within the window stated in the rules, and post the result publicly so entrants see it was actually resolved.
- One entry per person, stated up front.
- Never require a purchase to enter, never chain it to a follow-for-follow scheme, never ask anyone to tag themselves in a photo they're not in.

## 5. Crisis and negative-feedback playbook

One calm, honest public reply; move specifics to DM; never argue in the comments; escalate anything about food safety, refunds, or privacy beyond the general lines below to Abdullah.

### "AR didn't work on my phone"

> Sorry about that — that's on us to fix, not you. Quick check: are you on Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android)? Did you get to the dish page okay, or did it stop before that? If AR itself won't start, you can still turn the dish around in regular 3D on the same page while we look into it.
> *Maazrat, yeh humari taraf se masla hai. Bata dein kaunsa phone/browser use kar rahe hain.*

**Escalation:** log the device, browser, and where it failed; pass it to Abdullah/dev — this feeds the device QA matrix already planned for the build (CLAUDE.md Phase 9). Never promise a fix or a timeline you don't have.

### "The dish looked different [from what I saw on MENVA]"

> That shouldn't happen, and we're sorry it did — the whole point of the scan is that what you see is what comes out. Could you tell us which dish and roughly how it was different? We'll flag it to get checked.
> *Yeh nahi hona chahiye tha, maazrat. Kaunsa dish tha, thoda bata dein.*

**Never say:** don't blame the kitchen publicly, don't offer a refund or compensation (MENVA doesn't handle orders or payment — that's between the diner and the restaurant), don't argue the point. Escalate to Abdullah, who can raise it with the restaurant directly.

### Privacy questions ("are you tracking me", "what data do you collect")

> Good question. MENVA doesn't ask for your name, phone number, or any account — you just scan and browse. We only track simple, anonymous numbers for the restaurant's pilot (like how many people scanned, opened a dish, or used AR), never who you are.
> *Koi personal data nahi lete, sirf anonymous numbers restaurant ko dikhane ke liye.*

This is grounded in what's actually listed as pilot analytics in the brief (scans, dish opens, AR launches, lists shown to waiters) and the "no personal data" analytics design in the build plan — don't go further than this general line (e.g. specific claims about IP storage) without checking with Abdullah/dev first. Any request that goes beyond a general question — a deletion request, a formal complaint — escalates straight to Abdullah.

## 6. Weekly community routine

### Daily (about 15 minutes, ideally within a couple of hours of the 8:30pm post)

- Reply to every comment on today's post and the last 2–3 days' posts (5 min).
- Check and answer new DMs, especially anything containing "PILOT" (5 min).
- Note any recurring question not already covered in the reply bank, and anything that needs Abdullah (2 min).
- Quick look at today's post in Insights: reach, likes, comments, saves, shares (3 min).

### Weekly (an extra 15–20 minutes, once a week)

- Pull the week's Insights: which post and which pillar performed best (reach, saves, shares), profile visits, and link-in-bio clicks.
- Count DM volume containing "PILOT" as leads generated for the week.
- Add any new question type or joke pattern that came up to the reply bank.
- Roll anything for Abdullah into one place (this file's list below, or wherever the team tracks open items).

### Metrics to watch in Insights

- Reach and impressions per post, and per pillar (see-it-first, relatable, menu-truth, for-restaurants, behind-the-scan) — shows what diners vs. restaurant owners respond to.
- Saves — many captions explicitly say "save this," so saves are the clearest success signal for that content.
- Shares/sends — captions repeatedly ask people to "send this to a friend" or "send to the group chat."
- Comments and their mix (A/B votes, tags, skeptic questions, trolls).
- Profile visits → link-in-bio clicks (demo tries).
- DM volume, and how many contain "PILOT" (restaurant leads).
- Follower growth week over week.
- Best- and worst-performing post of the week, fed back into future calendar edits.
