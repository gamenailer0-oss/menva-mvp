# MENVA prospect list — Lahore research, not a call log

**This is a research list only. Nobody on it has been contacted. Nothing here implies any relationship with, or interest from, MENVA.** It was built the same way as the illustrative list in `sales-kit.md` section 1: from public restaurant review/listing sites and search results, not from calls, DMs, or visits. Every name needs to be verified before anyone reaches out — see "Needs Abdullah" at the end.

The pilot restaurant (the steakhouse in Gulberg III) is deliberately **not** on this list.

---

## 1. How this list was built

- Source tool: web search only (no restaurant was called, DM'd, messaged, or visited to build this list).
- Query set: Lahore steakhouses, fine dining, "aesthetic"/Instagrammable cafés, dessert spots, new openings, rooftop/grill restaurants, and area-specific searches for MM Alam Road/Gulberg, DHA, Johar Town, Model Town and Bahria Town — matching the ICP and the area order in `sales-kit.md` section 1.
- Every row in `prospects.csv` carries the source URL the name and description came from. Where a search's synthesized answer didn't specify an area, address, Instagram handle, follower count, or menu format, that field was left **blank** or marked **unknown** rather than guessed — per `BRIEF.md`: no invented stats, handles, follower counts, or names.
- A handful of names (El Momento, Café Aylanto, Spice Bazaar, The Urban Eatery, English Tea House, Sweet Affairs, Meat The Cheese, The Brasserie, Chow Ming) were already in `sales-kit.md` section 1's illustrative list. They're repeated here with the same citations so the full ~50-name list lives in one place; they're flagged in the `notes` column.
- Two domains that came up repeatedly in search results (reservekaru.com, elmomento.pk, padelcafe.pk, marketrates.pk, etc.) could not be opened directly with the page-fetch tool in this session (network policy blocked them); their content is cited exactly as the search tool surfaced it, with the URL given so Abdullah — or a future session with fetch access — can open the actual page and check it. Treat every listicle-derived fact as **unverified until someone opens the real page or visits in person.**
- No Instagram handle, follower count, phone number, or person's name was invented. Where a source didn't state one, the field is blank. `approx_followers` is blank across the whole list — no source in this research pass stated a specific restaurant's follower count with a date; the "Lahore food influencer" searches turned up personal food-blogger accounts, not restaurant accounts, so those numbers were left out entirely rather than attached to the wrong entity.
- `menu_has_photos` is `unknown` for every row. No source in this pass described a specific restaurant's actual printed or digital menu format (text vs. photo). This has to be checked in person or on the restaurant's own site/Instagram before using it in a pitch.

## 2. Prioritisation logic

Priority follows the ICP in `sales-kit.md` section 1, not review-site star ratings:

- **A (16 restaurants)** — best first-call fit: steakhouse/premium grill or fine dining with a described hero-dish moment (steak, teppanyaki, rooftop centrepiece), or a **new opening** (no legacy menu to fight), generally in the Gulberg/MM Alam/DHA cluster the sales-kit puts first. These are the restaurants worth Abdullah's first walk.
- **B (15 restaurants)** — solid single-criterion fit: photogenic cafés and dessert spots (category 3 in the ICP) where the source is clearly about visual/Instagram appeal, plus a couple of premium-casual spots outside the core cluster. Good second-pass targets, especially for building the "founding partner" story in newer areas.
- **C (19 restaurants)** — area-coverage or lower-confidence entries: thinner sourcing (a single generic listicle), unclear whether it's table-service or fast-casual, a multi-branch chain likely to need head-office approval (Chow Ming — see the sales-kit screening criteria on multi-branch chains), or a category that needs an in-person check before it's worth a pitch (e.g. a tea/music venue where the food menu itself isn't confirmed).

Every row also needs the sales-kit's 5-point screening check (table service, a hero dish, decent wifi, an active Instagram, one reachable decision-maker) applied **in person** — this list only gets a lead to "worth walking in," never to "worth calling."
