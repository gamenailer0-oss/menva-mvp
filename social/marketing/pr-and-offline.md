## 1. PR angles (8)

These are angles, not headlines — written for a Lahore tech/business or food journalist to see the story in one line.

1. **"See it before you order it": a Lahore startup lets diners preview food at true size before it arrives.** The core product hook — QR on the table opens a real 3D scan of the dish, then AR places it life-size on the diner's own table. Framing: solves the universal "the menu photo lied" complaint.
2. **No AI food photos, ever — a Lahore food-tech startup's stance in an AI-image era.** A counter-narrative story as AI-generated food imagery spreads on menus and delivery apps. MENVA's rule (real 3D scans only, confirmed in `BRIEF.md`) is a clean contrarian angle for a tech-ethics or "AI backlash" piece.
3. **A founder story: Abdullah and why he built AR menus in Lahore, not Karachi or Islamabad.** Standard founder-profile angle for outlets like Profit or TechJuice's startup features. Needs Abdullah's own account of the "why" and "why now" — not to be invented here.
4. **Tech for restaurants, not tech for delivery apps.** Most Pakistani food-tech coverage is about delivery (Foodpanda, cloud kitchens). MENVA is dine-in infrastructure — a different, under-covered category. Angle for TechJuice/ProPakistani's startup verticals.
5. **No app, no download: why MENVA works entirely in Safari and Chrome.** A technical/product-design angle — relevant to any outlet covering mobile web vs. native app trends, and useful for a "how it's built" sidebar.
6. **The waiter stays in charge: an AR menu that doesn't try to replace ordering or staff.** Positions MENVA against the "AI replaces jobs" narrative — it hands the waiter a clearer list, it doesn't take orders or payment. Good for a hospitality-industry or labour-angle piece.
7. **Pilot analytics: what actually happens when diners can see a dish before ordering.** Once [pilot restaurant]'s pilot analytics exist (scans, dish opens, AR launches — see `BRIEF.md`), this becomes a data-story angle: "we measured it." Cannot be pitched until real numbers exist and are approved for release.
8. **Lahore's restaurant scene as a testbed for AR retail tech.** A wider trend piece: Lahore's fine-dining growth + slow-wifi realities + WebAR maturing enough to work on mid-range Android. Good for Aurora (Dawn) or a business-trends feature rather than a straight product story.

## 8. University ambassador program

**Structure**

- One ambassador per partner university to start (e.g. LUMS, FC College, University of Lahore — all identified as having active food-festival culture in this research).
- Term: one semester, renewable.
- Core responsibilities: help staff campus activations (Section 6, #5 and #12), post 2–3 organic social pieces per semester about trying MENVA, refer their food society or event organisers for co-branded stalls, gather informal feedback from peers.
- Light-touch — a few hours a month, not a job.

**Incentive options (for Abdullah to choose from, not commitments made here)**

- A fixed small stipend per semester.
- Free/comped meals at partner restaurants when accompanying a demo.
- A "Founding Ambassador" credit/title usable on their CV or LinkedIn.
- First access to any MENVA merchandise or campaign content.
- A revenue-share or referral bonus tied to restaurants they help bring in as leads (only if Abdullah wants a paid-referral structure).

**Sign-up form questions**

1. Name, university, degree program, year.
2. Phone number and email.
3. Instagram/TikTok handle (if applicable — used for content collaboration, not required).
4. Which food society, club, or event team (if any) are you involved with?
5. Have you worked a campus stall, demo booth or event activation before? Briefly describe.
6. Why does MENVA interest you? (short answer)
7. How many hours a month could you realistically give this?
8. Which of these would matter most to you: stipend, free meals, CV credit, content collab, referral bonus? (rank or select)
