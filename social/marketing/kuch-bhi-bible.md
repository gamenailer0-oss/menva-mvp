# Kuch Bhi: character bible

> "Kuch bhi?"

## 1. The idea
**Kuch Bhi is the little creature that appears every time someone at the table says "kuch bhi, jo tum log lo".** It feeds on indecision, grows with every "pata nahi", and keeps the group staring at the menu for 40 minutes. The moment someone actually sees the dish, Kuch Bhi pops.

Why it works:
- **Everyone has said it.** Every friend group, every family dinner, every first date has a "kuch bhi" moment. Nobody has to be taught the joke.
- **It is MENVA's real enemy, without being food.** The problem MENVA solves is not knowing what you will get. Kuch Bhi is that feeling with eyes.
- **The product is the cure, never the hero.** Kuch Bhi pops when the dish is seen. We never have to explain the product; the pop explains it.
- **The catchphrase is free.** People already say it daily. Every time they do, they think of the blob.

## 2. Look

### Shape
- A soft, rounded blob, a little wider at the bottom like a drop of cream that has settled. About 1 : 1.1, width to height.
- No arms or legs by default. When it needs to act, two small nubs push out of its body and sink back in.
- **Question-mark antenna:** a single thin stalk on top that curls into a "?". It is the silhouette test: if the "?" reads, the character reads.
- **Eyes:** two solid dot eyes, set slightly wide and slightly low (cute, a bit sleepy). A small white catch-light in each.
- **Mouth:** usually none. A tiny "o" when it speaks, a small wavy line when it is unsure. Never teeth.
- **Surface:** soft toon shading (3 tones, like the dessert film), a thin warm ink outline, a soft contact shadow.

### Colour (from the brand palette)
| Use | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Body (default) | Cream | `#FFF4E6` |

### Size rule
Kuch Bhi's size is the group's indecision.
- **Small (pebble):** one "kuch bhi" said.
- **Medium (cushion):** "pata nahi, tum batao" goes round the table once.
- **Large (beanbag):** 20 minutes, three menus, nobody has decided.
- **Huge (fills the frame):** someone says "acha ghar chalte hain".

It never grows when someone is decisive. It shrinks a little every time someone asks a clear question.

### Silhouette test
At thumbnail size, on any background, it must read as "blob with a question mark". If the "?" is lost, the drawing fails.

### What it is NOT
- Not food. No crust, icing, steam, plate, bite marks or crumbs on it, ever.
- Not a ghost. It doesn't float in a scary way or turn see-through.
- Not mean. It never laughs at anyone or hurts anyone. It is cosy chaos.
- Not a mascot that sells. It never holds a phone up to camera or says "download" or "scan now".

## 3. Personality and voice
- **What it is:** cosy, sleepy, a little smug, very patient. It loves long menus and group chats with 47 unread messages.
- **What it wants:** to keep every decision open forever. Its happiest place is the moment before anyone orders.
- **What it fears:** a dish you can actually see. Clarity makes it nervous, then it pops.
- **Voice:** tiny, soft, slightly high, unhurried. It speaks in questions. It never shouts (Madam shouts).
- **Catchphrase:** **"Kuch bhi?"** Always with the question mark, always a little hopeful.

### Lines it says
- "Kuch bhi?"
- "Jo tum log lo."
- "Pata nahi... tum batao?"
- "Ek aur menu dekh lein?"
- "Kal wala hi le lo?"
- "Photo mein to bara lag raha tha..."
- "Abhi decide karna zaroori hai?"
- (just before popping) "Oh. Dikh gaya?"

### Lines it never says
- Anything decisive ("Yeh lo", "Final").
- Anything about MENVA, the app, scanning or ordering.
- Anything rude about anyone's body, food choices, money or family.

## 5. Its world
Kuch Bhi lives in the same universe as everything MENVA already posts.

- **The Khana Group (Ayesha, Sara, Hamza, Zain):** Kuch Bhi sits at their table every week. It grows fastest when Zain is choosing.
- **Zain, its best friend:** Zain has never decided anything. He carries Kuch Bhi in his hoodie pocket.
- **Madam, its nemesis:** when Ayesha puts her glasses on, Kuch Bhi shrinks. "Kuch bhi? KUCH BHI? Class se bahar!" Madam can't pop it; she can only scare it small.
- **Hamza:** feeds it by ordering "teen platter" without looking.
- **Sara:** believes every menu photo, so Kuch Bhi likes sitting on her shoulder.
- **Jumbo (the menu-photo burger), its frenemy:** they are both liars of a kind, so they get along. They are never in a gag that makes food the character; Jumbo stays a guest.
- **Table 12, its home:** the same wooden table in a Lahore restaurant, where most episodes happen. The table has a QR sticker that Kuch Bhi avoids looking at.

## 6. The story loop and rules
**Every appearance follows the same loop:**
1. **Indecision:** someone says "kuch bhi", and Kuch Bhi appears.
2. **Growth:** "pata nahi" goes round, and it grows.
3. **The dish is seen:** someone sees what the dish actually looks like.
4. **The pop:** soft pop, a floating "?", and the group finally orders. Relief.

**Rules:**
- MENVA is never the hero. The clarity is. Most posts don't show the product at all; when they do, it's only the moment of seeing.
- Kuch Bhi never dies, never gets hurt and is never "defeated". It pops and comes back next time someone says "kuch bhi".
- No episode ends with Kuch Bhi winning for good. The table always eats.
- It's always the same character. No evil twin and no "Kuch Bhi 2.0".

## 7. Rewatch mechanics
This is what makes people come back and look again.

### Hidden in every post
A tiny Kuch Bhi hides in every new MENVA post: meme, chat, film, Story.
- **Size:** 40 to 90 px on a 1080 px frame. It should be findable, not obvious.
- **Where it may hide:** behind a glass, inside a menu, on a lamp, in the group-chat avatar row, in the corner of a phone screen, in a film's background.
- **Where it never hides:** over copy, over faces, over the logo, over the real dish scan.
- **In films:** it appears for under one second at a moment we note internally, so the comments find it.

### "Kuch Bhi kahan hai?"
- Every Friday caption asks "Kuch Bhi kahan hai?".
- The first comment with the right spot (or timestamp) gets a reply and a shout-out in Stories.
- Monthly: the best finder gets the sticker pack early.

### Weekly mood drop
Every Monday, one mood gets its own post: the mood, its trigger, and "tag the friend who is this mood".

### Sticker pack
A sticker set of all the moods, for followers who DM "kuch bhi". It spreads the character into every group chat, which is exactly where the "kuch bhi" moment happens.

## 8. Format ideas

## 9. Do's and don'ts
**Do**
- Keep it soft, cosy and slow. It is the calmest thing in the frame.
- Start every bit from a real line people say at the table.
- Let it lose gently. It always pops, and always comes back.
- Hide it in every post once the series starts.

**Don't**
- Make it food, or put food on it.
- Use emoji or em dashes in its copy.
- Use brands, real people, prices or unprovable claims in its posts.
- Write or hint at the treasure hunt answer.
- Make jokes about religious days, national days, bodies, money trouble or family conflict. On religious and national days, Kuch Bhi takes the day off.
- Use any art that isn't ours. Every drawing, model and sound is original.
