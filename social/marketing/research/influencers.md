## 2. MENVA micro-influencer seeding program

This targets the **nano and micro tiers** above (roughly under 100k). Micro/nano creators in food content typically have higher engagement rates and a more locally-trusted voice than the macro tier, and Lahore's food-influencer scene clearly has an active nano/micro layer (several accounts under 25K with genuine Lahore-specific content, per the table above).

### Selection criteria

- **Based in Lahore, or posts Lahore-specific food content regularly** (not a Karachi/Islamabad account that occasionally visits).
- **Real engagement over raw follower count.** Heepsy's own data above shows engagement rates as low as 0.4% on some accounts with 80k+ followers — a smaller account with visibly real comments (not bot replies) and consistent posting is worth more than a bigger, quieter one.
- **Content style matches MENVA's brand register**: calm, warm, a little playful — not loud/prank-style content. Avoid creators whose main format is aggressive clickbait or controversy-bait.
- **Posts real dish/restaurant content, not just aesthetic café shots** — MENVA's pitch (photo-real 3D, true-to-size AR) needs someone who'll actually engage with *how the food looks*, since that's the whole point of the product.
- **No prior brand-safety issues** — nothing that would embarrass a first-pilot-restaurant relationship (BRIEF.md is explicit the pilot restaurant's name must never appear in public copy without founder sign-off, so this also applies double to influencer content shot there).

### The offer

- **An invite to visit the pilot restaurant and try the MENVA AR menu firsthand** — scan the table QR, see the real dish scans in AR, use "Show the waiter."
- **No payment, no guaranteed posting, no promised reach or deliverables required from them.** This is a low-pressure "come see it, post if you genuinely want to" seeding invite, not a paid campaign — because BRIEF.md rules require nothing to be promised that isn't real, and payment/partnership terms are a founder decision (see "Needs Abdullah" below).
- **What they get concretely**: a reserved table time, a walkthrough of the AR feature (so they don't have to figure it out on camera), and — if the pilot restaurant agrees — their meal for that visit. (Whether the restaurant comps the meal is the restaurant's/Abdullah's call, not something to promise in outreach — flagged below.)
- **What we do NOT offer**: cash payment, a set number of "required" posts, exclusivity, or any claim about their reach/results. No pay-for-post terms should be discussed by anyone other than Abdullah.

### The brief to send (once someone has agreed to visit)

Keep it one page, plain language:

1. **What MENVA is** (use only the confirmed facts from BRIEF.md: QR-to-AR dish menu, no app, real 3D scans, true-size AR placement, "Show the waiter," works in-browser).
2. **What to expect at the visit**: scan the table QR, browse 2-4 dishes in AR (steak board and steak sandwich are nameable; the other two dishes should be referred to descriptively, e.g. "a couple of other dishes," never guessed at by name), try placing a dish on their own table.
3. **No script, no required angle.** They can film however they want, or not film at all.
4. **One ask**: if they do post, please don't state or imply a price, don't claim inclusions MENVA doesn't guarantee, and tag @[MENVA's actual handle — confirm with Abdullah] if they're comfortable doing so. Optional, not mandatory.
5. **One thing to avoid**: no AI/CGI recreation of the AR effect for the post — ask them to film the real screen, since MENVA's own rule is real scans only, and a faked-looking recreation would undercut the product's actual selling point (this is honest, working AR, not a mockup).

### Dos and don'ts for whoever runs this program

**Do**
- Personalize every outreach message — reference something specific from their actual recent content (a dish they reviewed, a restaurant they visited).
- Follow up once, politely, after about a week of silence. Then stop.
- Say clearly and upfront that this is unpaid, so nobody feels misled partway through.
- Let them keep full creative control over anything they post.
- Track outcomes (see measurement section) so the next round of outreach can be evidence-based, not guesswork.

**Don't**
- Don't promise payment, "future paid campaigns," or any specific reach/results.
- Don't ask them to state a price, describe pilot inclusions, or name the pilot restaurant in ways the founder hasn't approved.
- Don't send bulk, copy-pasted DMs — Lahore's food-creator community is small enough that generic outreach gets noticed and shared as a bad example.
- Don't contact anyone whose handle/follower count isn't confirmed by a real source (see the "not actionable" list above) — that's a guess, not research.
- Don't offer or discuss payment/PR terms — that decision sits with Abdullah, not with whoever is running outreach day-to-day.

### How to measure it

Since there's no paid-tracking infrastructure for this (it's an organic seeding program), measure what's actually observable:

- **Sends**: number of outreach DMs sent, and to whom (log handle, tier, date sent, response).
- **Response rate**: how many replied at all, and how many accepted the visit invite.
- **Visits completed**: how many creators actually came in and used the AR menu.
- **Organic posts**: did they post (Story, Reel, feed post)? Track the post URL.
- **Saves and shares on any resulting post** (visible via the creator's own Instagram/TikTok insights if they're willing to share a screenshot — not something MENVA can pull directly without their permission).
- **Profile visits / link clicks to menva-ar.netlify.app** in the period right after a post goes up, cross-referenced against MENVA's own site analytics (if/when that's wired up) — a rough before/after comparison, not a precise attribution, since there's no UTM-tagging infrastructure described in BRIEF.md.
- **Pilot restaurant's own MENVA analytics** (scans, dish opens, AR launches — already part of the product per BRIEF.md) around the dates of any influencer visit, as a directional signal only, not a causal claim, since foot traffic has many drivers.

None of this requires paid tools — it's log-keeping plus asking creators directly for their own numbers if they're comfortable sharing.

## 3. Ready outreach DM (English + Roman Urdu)

Two short variants — pick whichever fits the platform (Instagram DM vs. a slightly warmer voice-note-style message). Both are placeholders for Abdullah/whoever sends them to personalize with a specific detail about the creator's own content before sending — don't send verbatim as a form letter.

**English:**

> Hi [name], love your [specific dish/restaurant] post from [reference something real] — genuinely made me want to go.
>
> I'm with MENVA — we've built a QR-to-AR menu for restaurants in Lahore. You scan the table QR, see a real 3D scan of the dish, and place it at true size on your own table before you order. No app, works straight in your phone browser.
>
> We're inviting a few Lahore food folks whose taste we actually trust to come try it at [pilot restaurant name — internal only, confirm with Abdullah before naming publicly]. No strings — no payment, no required post, just come see it and eat. If you like it and want to share something, great; if not, still good to meet you.
>
> Would a visit this week or next work for you?

**Roman Urdu:**

> Hi [naam], [restaurant/dish ka reference] wali post bohat achi thi — dekh ke jaane ka mann ho gaya.
>
> Main MENVA se hoon — humne Lahore ke restaurants ke liye ek QR-to-AR menu banaya hai. Table pe QR scan karo, dish ka real 3D scan dekho, aur apni table pe true size mein place kar ke dekho — order karne se pehle. Koi app nahi, bas phone ke browser mein khul jaata hai.
>
> Hum kuch Lahore food creators ko invite kar rahe hain jinki taste pe humein bharosa hai — aap aa ke try karein. Koi payment ya required post nahi — bas aa ke dekhein aur khaayein. Agar pasand aaye aur share karna chahein to zaroor, warna bhi koi baat nahi, milna acha rahega.
>
> Is hafte ya agle hafte kabhi time hoga?
