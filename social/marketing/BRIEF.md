# Brief for everyone working on MENVA marketing

## What MENVA is (only these facts are true; don't add any)
- A QR-to-AR dish menu for restaurants in **Lahore, Pakistan**. A diner scans the QR on the table, the menu opens in the phone's browser (**no app**, works in **Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android**), taps a dish and sees a **real 3D scan** of the plate, then places it **on their own table in AR at true size** (it can't be pinch-resized in AR). Phones without AR can still turn the dish in 3D.
- It's built for slow restaurant wifi: the dish photo shows first, and the 3D follows.
- "**Show the waiter**": diners add dishes plus notes ("no onions") to a list and turn the phone to the waiter. There's **no online ordering or payment**; the waiter stays in charge.
- Restaurants get pilot analytics: scans, dish opens, AR launches, lists shown to waiters.
- **Pilot price: PKR 25,000** (Lahore). What it includes is NOT known, so never state inclusions.
- The first pilot restaurant exists, but **its name must never appear in public-facing copy** without the founder's OK. In internal docs, write "[pilot restaurant]".
- There are 4 real dish scans: a steak board, a steak sandwich, "the green plate" and "the trio". The last two dish names are unconfirmed, so never name them.
- No AI-generated food imagery, ever. Use real scans only.
- Website: menva-ar.netlify.app (a custom domain is coming). Brand line: "See it before you order it." / "See it on your table. Then order it." Founder: Abdullah.
- Brand look "A · Chili": cream paper #EFEBE2, ink #1A1714, chili red #B5371F, Instrument Serif headlines + DM Sans. Voice: calm, warm, a little playful, never cartoonish. Sentence case. **No emoji in brand copy.** English first, plus a natural Lahori Roman Urdu line.
