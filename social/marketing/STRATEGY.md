## The idea everything hangs on

**Pakistani food hype runs on doubt, resolved in public.** "Is it really that big?", "Is it worth the hype?", "Is this even real?" Crumble's weekly reveals, the Tim Hortons queue, "worth the hype?" vlogger reviews, the Dubai chocolate cut-open videos: all of them are people settling a doubt on camera ([research/pakistan-food-trends.md](research/pakistan-food-trends.md)).

MENVA is the honest answer to that doubt, **before** you order: the real dish, scanned, on your own table at true size. So every piece of marketing does one of two things:
1. **Raise the doubt** in a way Lahore recognises (menu anxiety, the portion surprise, the 2019 menu photo, the order you regretted), then
2. **Resolve it** with a real scan, in a line people repeat: **"Pehle dekho, phir order."**

Positioning: *the only menu in Lahore that shows you the real plate at its real size before you order* (no app, no AI food, the waiter stays in charge). See [research/competitors.md](research/competitors.md): no AR/3D dish competitor was found operating in Pakistan.

## Two audiences, one funnel

| | Diners (Gen Z, 18–30, Lahore) | Restaurants (owners, GMs, marketing managers) |
|---|---|---|
| Why they care | No surprises, no regret, a fun thing to show the table | Guests who order with confidence, plating that sells itself, staff time, real numbers |
| Where they are | Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, food vloggers | Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, walk-ins at 3–5 pm, LinkedIn, events |
| What we ask | Send, save, tag, comment A/B, try the demo | **DM "PILOT"** / WhatsApp "PILOT" → a 10-minute demo on their own table |
| Proof | The scans themselves | The demo on their own table, then pilot analytics |

Diner attention is what makes restaurants listen, and restaurant pilots are what give diners something to scan. Both loops feed each other.

|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (setup) | Server live in dry run, profile set up, highlights uploaded | Landing page live, WhatsApp Business set up, deck and one-pager filled in | ~4 hours once |
| 1–2 | Calendar starts (launch post #1). Stories run automatically. 2 no-filming Reels a week (reels.md NF1–NF5) | Build a list of 30 target restaurants (sales-kit.md §1). 10 cold DMs + 5 walk-ins a week | 30 min/day |
| 3–4 | **Sight test** campaign (posts #25 plus Reels). Reply bank in use | First demos. **Founding tables** framing if Abdullah picks it | 45 min/day |
| 5–8 | **Dish drop** weekly series (swap in trend-bank posts). First filmed Reels with friends | Pilot #2–#3. First LinkedIn build-in-public posts | 45 min/day |
| 9–12 | **Menu photos lie** (honest version). **First reactions** once a partner agrees to filming | Pilot results report for the first restaurant (sales-kit.md §9) → case study → PR push (pr-and-offline.md) | 45 min/day |

Paid ads only after organic shows which posts get sent and saved: boost the proven ones first (paid-ads.md, tier 1).

## What to measure (weekly, 10 minutes)

| Metric | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Sends per reach** (shares) | Instagram's strongest ranking signal | Insights, per post |
| Saves | Checklists and guides are working | Insights |
| Comments on A/B and "types of people" posts | Community is forming | Insights |
| **"PILOT" DMs + WhatsApps** | The number that pays the bills | The CRM sheet (sales-kit.md §8) |
| Demos booked → pilots signed | Sales conversion | CRM |
| Scans, dish opens, AR launches, lists shown to waiters at partner tables | Proof for the next restaurant | /stats pilot page |

Rule: every 4 weeks, make more of the top 3 formats by sends and cut the bottom 3.

## Non-negotiables (every piece, every channel)

Real scans only, never AI food. Never name or show Gauchos without Abdullah's OK. No invented numbers, results or testimonials: research numbers are cited as research, never as MENVA results. The only price is the PKR 25,000 pilot, and its inclusions are never stated until Abdullah confirms them. Don't name the two unconfirmed dishes. No emoji. English plus natural Roman Urdu. Consent for every face on camera. Details: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md), brand-book.md.
