## Checks that passed (no issues found)

For transparency, these specific items from the audit brief were checked in depth and found **compliant**:

- **Gauchos naming**: never named or shown in any public-facing file (landing page, captions, ad copy, DMs, press release, video scripts). Internal docs correctly use "[pilot restaurant]" or, where they do name Gauchos (`OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, `README.md`, `SETUP.md` — engineering/ops docs outside `social/marketing/`), it is consistent with `CLAUDE.md`, which already names Gauchos as the confirmed first client for the engineering build.
- **Banned dish words** (prawn, skewer, fajita, wrap) in calendar content: present only in the internal `slides[].dish` asset-ID field of `calendar.json`/`calendar-2.json` (e.g. `garlic-prawn-skewers`), never in any visible caption, headline, body or alt text — verified programmatically across all 144 posts in both calendars.
- **Price**: PKR 25,000 is the only price used anywhere, always restaurant-facing (audience `restaurants`), never appears in a diner-facing post — verified across both calendars.
- **Pilot inclusions**: never stated as fact anywhere; every file that raises the question ("what does the pilot include") answers with a deferral ("DM us, we'll walk you through it") rather than an invented answer.
- **DM keyword "PILOT"**: consistent everywhere it's used (organic captions, paid ads, WhatsApp, landing page).
- **Brand colours** (`#EFEBE2` / `#1A1714` / `#B5371F`) and both brand lines ("See it before you order it." / "See it on your table. Then order it."): consistent, verbatim, across every file, including `landing/page.html`.
- **Emoji**: none found in any actual brand copy; the one emoji hit (`brand-book.md:123`) is inside a "don't do this" example, correctly illustrating a banned pattern.
- **AI-generated food imagery**: no file claims or implies AI imagery is used; every file reinforces the opposite, consistently.
- **Post-number cross-references** (e.g. `paid-ads.md`'s "calendar post #13 'turn-it-around'", `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`'s "#57", `plan.md`'s "#23 how we scan", all of `plan-2.md`'s series call-outs) were checked against the `n` field in `calendar.json`/`calendar-2.json` — every reference resolves to the correct post ID.
- **Section cross-references** (e.g. `MORNING.md` → `sales-kit.md §6/§8`, `campaigns.md #4/#6`; `STRATEGY.md` → `brand-book.md` Section 8; `video-scripts.md`/`reels.md` internal section references) all resolve to the correct, existing sections.
- **Counted claims**: "30 Reel scripts" (30 found), "5 no-filming Reels" (5 found, remainder list is exhaustive and non-duplicated), "17 objections" (17 found), "20 LinkedIn posts" (20 found), "16 for-restaurants posts, ~22%" in `calendar-2.json` (16/72 = 22.2%, confirmed), "105 slides" in `calendar-2.json` (105 confirmed).
- **File references**: every markdown link and every backtick-quoted filename referenced across the audited files resolves to a real file in the repo (including `social/templates/story.html`, `design/MENVA.DESIGN.md`, `css/tokens.css`).
- **Markdown tables**: no column-count mismatches found in any audited file.
- **Risky-advice areas** (giveaway/contest law, filming consent, kite-flying/Basant, Ramadan sensitivity, political neutrality on national days, WhatsApp broadcast spam, Instagram promotion rules): all handled carefully and correctly — giveaways are gated behind a confirmed partner and cite Meta's actual promotion guidelines with a source URL and flag unresearched Pakistani promotion law explicitly; kite references are deliberately excluded per Punjab's kite-flying ban; Ramadan/iftar posts are respectful and non-mocking; Pakistan Day/Quaid-e-Azam Day posts are neutral and non-political; WhatsApp broadcast guidance is opt-in-only, frequency-capped and sourced; the cricket-jack campaign explicitly avoids gambling references, player likenesses and score predictions.
- **Unsourced numbers**: the document set is unusually disciplined — nearly every statistic carries an inline citation URL or an explicit "estimate"/"not verified" label, including deliberately weak/self-flagged ones (e.g. the Karachi wedding figure, the 820% tourism figure, the Oxford dessert study repeatedly labelled as someone else's result, never MENVA's).

## Summary

This is a well-disciplined, heavily cross-referenced document set — the vast majority of the brief's rules (no Gauchos naming, no unconfirmed dish names, single PKR 25,000 price restricted to restaurant audiences, no AI imagery, no emoji, sourced numbers, consent handling, legal/platform-policy awareness) are followed correctly and consistently across all 23 audited files, including the two calendars (144 posts total) and the public landing page.

Six issues were found, none of them severe:

1. **One real rule break** (High): `website.md` line 259 names the unconfirmed "green plate" dish by its actual working name ("garlic prawn skewers") in prose, and misdescribes the landing page's alt text as naming it too, when the alt text is correctly generic.
2. **Two undocumented scheduling exceptions** (Medium): `plan.md` and `plan-2.md` each state a blanket posting-time/posting-day rule that the actual `calendar.json`/`calendar-2.json` data quietly overrides (a Monday launch day in season one; a 16:30 PKT Ramadan time-shift in season two) without saying so — season two documents its own launch-day exception but not the Ramadan one; season one documents neither exception.
3. **One unconfirmed process promise in a live sales script** (Medium): `sales-kit.md` commits to a specific chef sign-off step that isn't established anywhere else as a confirmed pilot process.
4. **Two low/cosmetic items**: a stale count in a `pr-and-offline.md` section header, and dish-name words surviving in an image filename (not visible copy) on the landing page.

No broken tables, no dead internal links, no contradicting brand colours/lines/handle, and no diner-facing price leaks were found.
