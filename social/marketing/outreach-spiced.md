# Outreach, rewritten so it sounds like a person

These replace the scripts in `sales-kit.md` §4, which read like a template: flattery first ("one of the best feeds in Lahore"), dashes everywhere, the whole product in one breath, and a polite ask at the end. Owners in Lahore get these every week and delete them.

**What changes:**
1. **Open with one specific thing about their restaurant**, something only someone who looked would know: a dish they posted this week, the question in their comments, their Friday queue. That line is the only personalisation that counts. If you can't write it, don't send the message.
2. **One idea per message.** The first message never explains the whole product. It opens a loop.
3. **Show before you tell.** Attach `social/reels/out/for-restaurants.mp4` (14 s) or a 10-second screen recording of the demo on your own table.
4. **Short.** A DM should fit on one phone screen without scrolling. Write the way you'd text a friend's older brother: respectful, not stiff.
5. **Never flatter, never invent.** Say nothing about their numbers, never "guests are asking for this", and never name the pilot restaurant.

Fill in `[…]` before sending. If a message still reads fine with the brackets left in, it's too generic. Rewrite it.

## Instagram DM, cold (pick one opener)

**A. The comment-section opener** (use when their comments are full of "price?", "portion?", "is it enough for 2?"):
> Salam [Restaurant] team. Saw your [dish] post, and half the comments are "portion kitna hai?" / "2 logon ke liye kaafi hai?". We built something that answers exactly that at the table: the guest scans your table QR and the real dish shows up on their table, true size. 14-sec video attached. Worth a look?

**B. The menu-photo opener** (use when their menu or Google photos are old):
> Salam. Quick one about [Restaurant]: your [dish] on Instagram looks nothing like the photo on the table menu, and the Instagram one is way better. We put the real plate on the guest's table in 3D, straight from the QR, no app. Can I show you on one of your tables for two minutes, maybe [day] around 4?

**C. The owner-to-owner opener** (use for a new place in its first 3 months):
> Salam [Name]. Congrats on opening [Restaurant]. First months are when people decide if a place is "their" place. We make the menu show the actual dish on the guest's table in 3D, true size, from the table QR. I'm doing a small paid pilot in Lahore. 2-minute demo on your own table, your call after that. Kab theek rahega?

## Instagram DM, when they reply

> Great. I'll bring it on my phone and show it on your table, you don't need to set anything up. [Day] 4 pm or [day] 5 pm?

(Give two times, not "whenever suits you". Owners pick; they don't plan.)

## WhatsApp, first message (when someone passed you the number)

> Salam [Name], Abdullah here. [Who] gave me your number, said you're the one who decides on the menu side at [Restaurant]. I'll keep it short: we put the real dish on the guest's table in 3D, true size, from the QR. Video below. If it's interesting, I'll show it on your table in 2 minutes. If not, no worries at all.

## WhatsApp follow-up (day 3, only once)

> [Name], one more thing, then I'll stop: the waiter keeps the order. Guests just show the waiter their list on the phone, so nothing changes for your floor. Still happy to show you whenever there's a slow afternoon.

## WhatsApp, last touch (day 10)

> Last one from me, [Name]. If the timing's better after [Ramadan / wedding season / the new menu], just message "MENVA" and I'll come by. [Restaurant] ka [dish] still on my list either way.

## Walk-in, 3 to 5 pm (in Urdu first, because it's a conversation)

> "Salam, do minute chahiye bas. Main Abdullah hoon. Aap ke menu ka ek kaam aasaan karta hoon: guest QR scan karta hai aur dish asli size mein uske table pe aa jati hai, order se pehle. Yahin is table pe dikha deta hoon, kuch setup nahi."

If they say they're busy:
> "Koi baat nahi. Yeh card chhod jata hoon. Kal isi time aa jaun, ya WhatsApp pe video bhej dun?"

Then **stop talking and put the phone on the table.** The demo does the selling. The script only gets the phone onto the table.

## Voice note (20 seconds, not 40)

> "Salam [Name], Abdullah. Aap ke [dish] wali post dekhi, comments mein sab portion pooch rahe the. Hum ne aisa system banaya hai ke guest QR scan kare aur woh dish asli size mein apne table pe dekh le. Video bhej raha hoon. Pasand aaye toh do minute ke liye aa jata hoon."

## Email (only for groups and hotels; everyone else is on WhatsApp)

**Subject:** [dish] at true size, on your guest's table

> [Name],
>
> One question gets asked at every table in [Restaurant] every night: "how big is it?" We built the answer into the table QR. The guest sees your real dish, scanned in 3D, on their own table at true size, then orders with your waiter as usual. No app.
>
> 14-second video attached. The Lahore pilot is PKR 25,000. I'd rather show you than write more: 10 minutes on one of your tables, any afternoon next week.
>
> Abdullah, MENVA

## Comment-reply bank (for MENVA's own Instagram)

Reply within the first hour. Match the commenter's language. Never argue, never oversell.

| They comment | Reply |
|---|---|
| "Which restaurant is this?" | Pilot's in Lahore, launching with the restaurant soon. Follow along, we'll say the moment we can. |
| "Is this AI?" | Nope. The actual plate, scanned from every side. No AI food, ever. |
| "App ka naam?" | Koi app nahi. Table ka QR scan karo, browser mein khul jata hai. |
| "Doesn't work on my phone" | Which phone and browser? Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android works best. If you opened it inside Instagram, tap ••• then "open in browser". |
| "Price?" (from a diner) | Menu prices are the restaurant's. We just show you the plate before you order. |
| "Price?" (from a restaurant) | Lahore pilot is PKR 25,000. DM "PILOT" and we'll walk you through it. |
| "A" / "B" on this-or-that | Engage: "B gang is loud today" / "A walon, explain yourselves". |
| Tags a friend | "@friend you've been summoned." (Only when it fits the post.) |
| Something negative about a restaurant | Don't reply publicly beyond "Sorry to hear that." Never pile on. |
| "Lahore mein kahan?" | Coming to tables soon. Turn on post notifications, pehle tumhein pata chalega. |
