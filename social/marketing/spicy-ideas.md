# Bolder ideas (the spice list)

These go beyond the calendar: things people would talk about, not just scroll past. Each one says what it needs. Anything marked **Needs Abdullah** costs money, involves another person or venue, or goes public in a new way, so nothing here has been started.

| # | Idea | Why it works | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Khana kahan?** (built): a weekly group-chat sitcom with a fixed cast (Hamza, Anum, Zain, Sara, Ali bhai) as posts and Reels | A recurring cast brings people back. Everyone has a Hamza. "Tag your Hamza" gets shared without asking. | Nothing. It's ready: `content/chat-saga.json`, plus 4 Reels in `reels/out/` |
| 2 | **"Send us your Hamza moment"**: followers DM their real group-chat chaos. We rewrite it with the cast's names (never theirs) and post it as an episode | Content made by followers, and they feel part of the show | Moderation rule: remove real names, numbers and restaurant names. Abdullah approves each one |
| 3 | **The pehle dekho move**: a signature gesture for every Reel and creator (two fingers to the eyes, then point at the table) | Brands in Pakistan that spread have a move or a sound (think of the reveal-box unboxings). It's free to copy, and every copy is an ad. | Abdullah or a friend performs it on camera once |
| 4 | **Guess the size, weekly Story quiz**: a slider sticker for "how big is this board, from hand-size to laptop-size". The answer is revealed next day as an AR clip | Doubt answered in public is the hype engine from the research (`research/pakistan-food-trends.md`). The quiz costs nothing. | Stickers are added by hand in the app (the API can't add them) |
| 5 | **Blind order vs seen order**: two creators at the same table. One orders from the text menu, one sees the dish first. They compare when the food arrives | The format is built for sending and doesn't depend on any claim | **Needs Abdullah**: creators (`research/influencers.md`), the partner restaurant's OK, and paying for the meal |
| 6 | **Campus sight test**: a booth at a university food festival. Guess the size, then see it on the table in AR | Gen Z in person. Their Stories become our reach. | **Needs Abdullah**: a stall fee, the organiser, and a phone on a stand |
| 7 | **"Remix this with your group chat" Reels**: a chat Reel posted with an open invitation to remix | Remixes are native reach on Instagram | Nothing. Say it in the caption of `khana-kahan-ep1` |
| 8 | **Wrong answers only, Wednesdays**: a text post such as "Wrong answers only: what's in the 'chef's special'?" | The easiest comment prompt there is, and it builds a weekly habit | Nothing. It's a text post. Add it to the calendar when there's a gap |
| 9 | **The waiter's POV**: floor staff at the partner restaurant say the question they hear most, then the app answers it | Makes waiters the heroes (brand rule) and speaks to restaurant owners | **Needs Abdullah**: the partner's OK and every staff member's consent on camera |
| 10 | **Match-night order-before-the-toss Story**: a live Story at 7 pm on big match nights | Timing does the work, and the Reel already exists (`order-before-the-toss.mp4`) | Nothing. Post it by hand on match days |
| 11 | **The first 100 tables**: a counter in the bio and Stories of tables that have scanned during the pilot, using real numbers only from `/stats` | Shows progress in public without inventing anything | Only once the pilot is live, and only real numbers |
| 12 | **Founder's note in Urdu voice-over**: a 30-second Reel of Abdullah saying why he built it, in his own words and his own voice | People trust a face and a voice over a brand account. This is the least AI-looking thing we can post. | **Needs Abdullah**: record it on his phone. Script prompts are in `video-scripts.md` |

**Start with:** 1 (ready now), 7 and 8 (free, this week), then 4 once the account has a few hundred followers. Of the ideas that cost something, 5 gives the most for the money.
